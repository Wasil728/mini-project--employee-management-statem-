About Meridian Works

A small employee directory built to practise Django views, templates and static files.

Meridian Works is a made-up company. This site lets you browse its staff, open any profile, and see how employees are grouped by salary level.

How the site works

  • Employee records are a Python list in views.py. There is no database.
  • Each view passes that list to a template, and a {% verbatim %}{% for %}{% endverbatim %} loop builds one badge per employee.
  • Every employee has their own address, such as /employee/101/. The view reads the ID from the URL and finds the matching record.
  • Profile photos and the stylesheet are served from the static folder.
  • A small function turns each salary into Senior, Mid or Junior Level.

Pages

  • Home: summary and three featured employees
  • Employee list: every employee as a badge
  • Employee details: the full record for one person
  • About and Contact
